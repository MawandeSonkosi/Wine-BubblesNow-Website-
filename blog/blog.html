There's nothing better than getting out into the community and meeting the people who make Wine & Bubbles what it is. From the moment we arrived, we had the opportunity to connect with so many people who were curious about the Wine & Bubbles Now app. Whether they were hearing about us for the first time or already familiar with the brand, it was great to share what we do and welcome new users to our growing community.

This activation was made even more exciting thanks to our partnership with RYDAD. Their branded bikes helped us take the Wine & Bubbles experience beyond our stand and into the streets of Fourways, catching the attention of shoppers, commuters, and passers-by throughout the day.

For us, monthly activations are about more than promoting an app, they're about building relationships.

We're always looking for new ways to bring convenience closer to our customers, and these activations are one of our favourite ways to do that.
