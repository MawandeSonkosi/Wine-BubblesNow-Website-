Another Great Weekend, Another Successful Wine & Bubbles Activation

There's nothing better than getting out into the community and meeting the people who make Wine & Bubbles what it is. This past weekend, our team headed to Fourways for our monthly activation, and we couldn't have asked for a better day.

From the moment we arrived, we had the opportunity to connect with so many people who were curious about the Wine & Bubbles Now app. Whether they were hearing about us for the first time or already familiar with the brand, it was great to share what we do and welcome new users to our growing community.

Taking Our Brand Beyond the Activation

This activation was made even more exciting thanks to our partnership with RYDAD. Their branded bikes helped us take the Wine & Bubbles experience beyond our stand and into the streets of Fourways, catching the attention of shoppers, commuters, and passers-by throughout the day.

It's partnerships like these that allow us to reach more people in creative ways while making our brand more visible where it matters most.

Our monthly activations are about more than promoting an app — they're about building relationships.

More Than Just a Pop-Up

For us, monthly activations are about more than promoting an app — they're about building relationships.

Every conversation, every new download, and every interaction gives us the chance to understand what our customers are looking for while showing them just how easy it is to order their favourite wines and beverages through the Wine & Bubbles Now app.

We're always looking for new ways to bring convenience closer to our customers, and these activations are one of our favourite ways to do that.

Where Are We Heading Next?

If you missed us in Fourways, don't worry. We're already planning our next stop, and we'd love for you to join us.

Follow us on social media to stay up to date with our upcoming activations, exciting collaborations, and exclusive offers.

And if you haven't already, now's the perfect time to download the Wine & Bubbles Now app. With a wide range of premium wines and beverages available at your fingertips, ordering has never been easier.

We can't wait to see you at our next activation!
