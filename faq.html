1 What are your delivery hours?

We deliver daily from 9:00 AM to 11:00 PM throughout South Africa.

2 Do you offer same-day delivery?

Yes, we offer same-day delivery for orders placed before 8:00 PM.

3 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards, debit cards, and mobile payment methods.

4 Can I schedule delivery for a specific time?

Yes, you can schedule delivery during checkout for your preferred time slot.

5 What is your return policy?

Due to the nature of our products, we can only accept returns for defective or damaged items.

6 How do I track my order?

You will receive tracking information via email and SMS once your order is dispatched.

7 Do you deliver to all areas in South Africa?

Yes, we deliver throughout South Africa. Delivery times may vary by location.

8 What is the minimum order amount?

There is no minimum order amount for delivery.

9 Can I cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement. Contact us immediately for cancellations.

10 Do you offer wine recommendations?

Yes! Our wine experts are happy to provide recommendations based on your preferences.
